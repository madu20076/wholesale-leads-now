Questions About Selling to PenRunners

Do I need to make repairs before selling?

No. PenRunners Home Buyers purchases properties as-is, so you do not have to complete repairs or renovations before requesting an offer.

Am I required to accept the offer?

No. Our offer is provided without obligation. You can review it and decide whether it meets your needs.

How quickly can the sale close?

Closing timing depends on the property, title review, and your preferred schedule. We work with local title professionals to coordinate an appropriate closing date.

Do you charge realtor commissions?

PenRunners Home Buyers does not charge traditional realtor commissions when purchasing your property directly.
